sky reader · v0.4

What did you photograph?

Load a photo of the night sky. Meridian names the galaxies, nebulae, clusters and bright stars in the frame, marks where each one sits, and tells you how sure it is.

Works on a phone snapshot, a tracked exposure, or a photo through an eyepiece.
Blurry, over-exposed and city-sky frames are fine — you'll just get fewer objects back.

Positions are the model's read of your frame, not an astrometric solve.
